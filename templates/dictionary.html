Game Command Format

MOVE: "move [unit] [x] [y]" (e.g. "move p1_0 3 4" or "move alpha 3 4")

ATTACK: "attack [unit] [target_x] [target_y]" (e.g. "attack p1_1 5 2" or "attack beta 5 2")

Units can be identified by:

  • Unit IDs: p1_0 to p1_3 (Player 1), p2_0 to p2_3 (Player 2)
  • Commander Names: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta

Grid coordinates: x and y from 0 to 5
